SturdivantAI Lab / Independent Applied AI Research

Findings become prototypes. Prototypes become findings.

SturdivantAI Lab builds agentic multi-agent systems, evaluates models in regulated environments, and publishes from production. Each release is gated by a Large Language Model as a Judge (LLM-as-a-Judge) validation harness before it ships. The work is open where it can be. The standards are high where they must be.

01 / ABOUTFOUNDER-AS-SCIENTIST

The lab exists to compress the distance between an algorithm on paper and an algorithm in production.

SturdivantAI Lab is the research practice of Edidiong Umoh, Cloud AI Engineer and Master of Research (M.Res) in Artificial Intelligence candidate at Capitol Technology University. Useful methods can take years to migrate from a paper to an operating system that people rely on. The lab treats that lag as its core problem: research findings are reimplemented to production standard, gated behind a Verification and Validation (V&V) pass scored by an LLM-as-a-Judge harness, and shipped. The failure cases and evaluation traces that come back set the next research question.

Day to day, that means machine learning and MLOps pipelines, Large Language Model (LLM) tuning, model quantization and compression for edge deployments, open-source model installation and serving, and cloud orchestration across AWS and GCP.

Affiliations: IEEE Computer Society, GDG Annapolis, CTU Cyber Lab, Upwardly Global, AWS Nonprofit Community Group.

  • VISIONAn autonomous future where the friction between artificial intelligence theory and production engineering is eliminated: a self-evolving loop of empirical discovery.
  • MISSIONTranslate hidden algorithmic breakthroughs into battle-hardened, modular agentic systems. Design rigorous verification and telemetry frameworks that expose deep system edge cases. Feed those empirical insights back to the scientific community through peer-reviewed research.
  • AX.01Every paper we publish maps to a deployed capability within two quarters. We track paper-to-prototype latency per project and publish it.
  • AX.02We don't call a prototype done until it survives a production-grade V&V pass. Every release ships with its judge-harness evaluation report.
  • AX.03Our repositories hold to five practices: structure, automation, repeatability, testability, maintainability. Audit them yourself.

04 / CAPABILITIESRESEARCH CAPABILITIES

What the lab does

Agentic systems

Multi-agent engines and orchestrators with typed state, write-ahead journaling, and judge-gated transitions. Not conversational layers over a hosted API.

LLM evaluation

LLM-as-a-Judge harnesses and V&V gates that score releases against defined rubrics before anything ships.

Model compression

Quantization-aware training, distillation, and precision engineering. The optimization target: minimum bits, preserved reasoning.

Cloud AI engineering

Production systems on AWS Bedrock and Vertex AI, designed for recovery under real network conditions rather than demo conditions.

Regulatory AI

Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) verification and validation, and evaluation evidence built to survive an audit. Working frameworks:
